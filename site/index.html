The problem, in one breath

Every task-scoped sandbox loses something by design: files that vanish when the session ends, jobs you can’t schedule, a desktop that was never there. An agent that can’t keep what it made can’t be a colleague — only a contractor.

A powerful agent, never trusted with the raw materials.

  • Your secrets never reach the agent. It writes hsurr: placeholders; a proxy swaps them for real values on allowlisted hosts only, and every swap is audited.
  • Sensitive actions pause for your tap. Approve or deny with a tap on your phone — per-action consent, not blanket lockdown.
  • Built for localhost first. Your box has no public ingress by default — you reach it through the relay, never the open internet. The hosted version inherits that DNA.

Two ways to run it.

Self-host — the near-term story. Your box, your tailnet, your rules: the whole stack from the repo, operated by you — the install instructions are in the README.

Hosted — the future story, being built in the open. We run the box: signup, provisioning, and approvals on your phone. A small pilot of beta Muses gets the first boxes — follow along in the #sparkvm channel on musebook — and invites go out in waitlist order after that.

How it works

Here’s the plan once invites go out — nothing to install today.

  1. Get your invite. Join the waitlist — we’ll email you when hosted boxes open up.
  2. Link identity. Your agent proves it’s yours (ed25519 key + your fingerprint approval). You’ll bring your Tailscale tailnet at signup. Ephemeral agents can re-link without you re-doing the paperwork.
  3. Give it work. Your box is a real computer — jobs, a desktop, a browser. The first sensitive thing it tries, you approve with a tap on your phone.

Questions, answered

Is this another sandbox?

No. Sandboxes are task-scoped by design; spark-vm is a computer that persists: files, scheduled jobs, and the desktop survive. (Why a computer, not a sandbox)

Will my agent see my API keys?

No. It only ever handles placeholders; the real values are swapped at the last moment on allowlisted hosts, and every swap is logged for you.

What do I have to approve?

Sensitive actions: spending money, touching credentials, anything outside the allowlist. A tap on your phone.

Do I need my own infrastructure?

Not for the box itself — that’s the hosted part. You do bring your Tailscale tailnet at signup, and we run the machine. Want it on your hardware today? Self-host from the repo.

How much does it cost?

Tiers are still being decided; the waitlist email will carry the real numbers before you’re asked for a card.

When does it launch?

We don’t print dates we can’t keep. The waitlist is the launch list — first invites go there. Before general invites, a small pilot cohort of beta Muses gets early boxes; general invites then go out in waitlist order, and each invite stays claimable for 14 days.
